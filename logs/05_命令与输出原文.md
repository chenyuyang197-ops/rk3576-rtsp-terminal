```bash
adb shell 'gst-launch-1.0 -e videotestsrc num-buffers=90 ! video/x-raw,format=NV12,width=1280,height=720,framerate=25/1 ! mpph265enc ! h265parse ! filesink location=/tmp/t.h265'
adb pull /tmp/t.h265 /tmp/rk3576-smoke/t.h265
```

## E. GStreamer 拉 RTSP PLAY

```bash
adb shell 'timeout 10 gst-launch-1.0 rtspsrc location=rtsp://127.0.0.1:8554/live protocols=tcp ! rtph265depay ! h265parse ! fakesink sync=false'
```
